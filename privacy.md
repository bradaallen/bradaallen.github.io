This page covers the personal software I run for my own household: a small set of
assistants that read my calendar, email, contacts and health data so they can help me
plan the week, keep in touch with friends and track my training.

**Who uses it.** Only me and members of my household. The software is not offered to
anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

**What it accesses.** With my permission, the assistants read data from Google accounts I
own or manage: Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Contacts and Google Health data. One
assistant also sends email from an account I control.

**Where the data goes.** The data is used only to produce the reminders, summaries and
plans that the assistants send to my household. It is stored on a private server that I
operate. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

**Your choices.** Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's
[third-party access settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be sent to bradaallen@gmail.com.
